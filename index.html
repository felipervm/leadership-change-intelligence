03 / How it works

Trace the claim. Then the work.

The aim isn't automation for its own sake. It's a repeatable way for an analyst to explain what changed, what is known, what is missing, and why the next check matters.

01 / Capture

Source-first records

Extract names and role selections from the dated HHS announcement. Retain direct official links and publication dates for every change.

02 / Verify

Separate evidence from assumptions

Cross-check names and current organizational placement against the FDA's September chart. Flag predecessor history and effective-date details for research instead of guessing.

03 / Prioritize

Explain every score

Apply a documented heuristic that considers the type of task, hypothesized organizational impact and evidence gaps. No unvalidated claims of time saved.

What would make this operationally useful? Historical snapshots, true dependency edges, reviewer effort estimates, client-criticality signals and outcomes of completed reviews. This small case shows a possible method, not a validated production prioritization system.

01 / DATA RISK

A URL isn't a snapshot.

The FDA organization-chart webpage changes over time: the page now displays Oct 13, not Sep 16. Historical comparisons require dated sources and a recorded observation date.

What remains unknown: These sources do not establish the exact personnel-effective date. A recorded selection or published chart is not automatically the date someone formally began serving.

05 / Outside the FDA

Not every appointment
is a replacement.

A second official announcement tests the event model against a different type of organizational change.

SECOND CASE

8 new members

On September 17, HHS named eight new members of the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force, including a designated Chair.

DIFFERENT SIGNAL

Addition ≠ departure

A membership announcement does not prove eight predecessors left. A data team should not infer removals or effective dates without separate evidence.

DATA OPERATIONS DECISION

Type the change first.

Different event types should trigger different verification checklists: appointment status, new office, or incoming member.
